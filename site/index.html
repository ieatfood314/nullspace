Nullspace puts dictation, screenshots and voice-note tasks on one key: the Essential key, or any key you pick. Free, open source, on any Android 12+ phone.

One key does it all

Hold

Dictate into any text field. It runs on the phone: about 0.65 s for 7 s of speech on a CMF Phone 2 Pro.

Press

Keep what’s on screen: a screenshot and its text.

Press, then hold

Record a voice note. It becomes a task.

Double press

Open Nullspace.

Say it. It’s filed.

No “analyzing” spinner. A voice note becomes a real task or reminder straight away.

Nullspace isn’t made by or connected to Nothing. Essential Space and the Essential key are Nothing’s names, used here to compare.

Works alone. Better with a hub.

Just the phone

No server. A built-in parser files your captures offline.

Add a free Groq API key (any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works) and an LLM files them instead. It also tidies rambling dictation: it cuts repeats and false starts and keeps your words.

With a hub

A small Python server on your own machine.

Tasks, notes, captures and places sync across your phone and the web, for real.

Your voice stays put

  • Audio never leaves the phone.
  • Screenshots are copied only to your own hub, and only on Wi-Fi.
  • Only text goes to the LLM you chose.
  • You set a cap for mobile data.
  • Sharing anything for training is off until you turn it on.

On your desktop too

Nullspace for Linux puts the same key on your keyboard: hold to dictate, press to keep what’s on screen, press then hold for a voice note. Speech runs on your graphics card through Squelch, and it all lands in the same list as your phone.

On Arch: yay -S nullspace. Just want dictation? Squelch works on its own.

Get it

Download the APK

Or add github.com/ieatfood314/nullspace to Obtainium and updates come to you.

Needs Android 12 or newer. The first launch downloads the speech model once (about 600 MB).

On a Nothing or CMF phone, turn off Nothing’s own Essential apps first. The Android readme has the commands.
